# Acme Corp Remote and Flexible Work Policy

At Acme Corp, we understand that modern work environments thrive when flexibility and trust are balanced with personal accountability, clear communication, and high operational standards. We recognize that remote working empowers team members to focus, manage their personal and professional commitments effectively, and eliminate stressful daily commutes. This Remote and Flexible Work Policy outlines the rules, expectations, operational guidelines, and technological requirements that enable seamless collaboration across distributed and hybrid teams at Acme Corp.

Our company culture is built upon trust, mutual respect, and delivery of meaningful outcomes. Whether an employee works from an Acme Corp designated office, a home workspace, or another approved remote location, the expectation for exceptional performance, professional integrity, and active collaboration remains identical.

## Purpose and Guiding Principles

The objective of this policy is to establish standard practices for remote working arrangements that support employee autonomy while safeguarding team cohesion, productivity, client responsiveness, and information security. Remote work is a cooperative arrangement between Acme Corp and its workforce, designed to optimize individual productivity while ensuring that business objectives and team milestones continue to be met without compromise.

Flexible working is governed by three fundamental principles: transparent accountability, consistent availability during team interaction windows, and the rigorous protection of corporate assets and customer confidential data. Eligibility for remote work is contingent upon job responsibilities that can be successfully executed outside of a physical Acme Corp facility without degrading team output or client service levels.

## Weekly Remote Work Allowance

Under the Acme Corp hybrid work model, eligible regular team members are granted Up to 3 remote days per week, with the remaining workdays spent collaborating in person at their designated regional Acme Corp office facility. This hybrid arrangement balances the undeniable benefits of independent, focused work at home with the invaluable social connection, creative brainstorming, and spontaneous mentorship that occurs during in-person office interactions.

The specific days designated for remote work should be coordinated and agreed upon with the employee's direct line manager to prevent staffing shortfalls and ensure adequate team presence during key operational meetings. Certain departments or project teams may establish standardized anchor days—such as Tuesdays and Thursdays—during which all team members are expected to attend the office in person for cross-functional workshops, sprint planning, and client reviews.

## Working From Abroad and International Travel

Acme Corp appreciates that employees may wish to combine personal travel, family visits, or cultural exploration with remote work. In support of this modern lifestyle, working from another country is allowed up to 30 days per year with manager approval. This international remote work privilege allows personnel to extend international trips or visit family living overseas while continuing to perform their daily duties without taking unpaid leave.

Because working across international borders introduces complex tax, legal, regulatory, and data privacy implications, employees seeking to work from abroad must submit a formal request via the HR portal at least thirty days prior to departure. The employee must obtain explicit written approval from both their direct manager and the Acme Corp Global Mobility and Compliance team. The employee is solely responsible for ensuring they possess valid travel documentation, appropriate tourist or work visas, and reliable high-speed internet access. Furthermore, employees traveling abroad must strictly adhere to Acme Corp IT security protocols, avoiding untrusted public Wi-Fi networks and utilizing corporate VPN services at all times.

## Core Collaboration Hours and Availability

To facilitate smooth teamwork, prevent communication bottlenecks, and enable timely synchronous decision-making across varied domestic time zones, Acme Corp enforces standard daily core collaboration hours. Specifically, core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone, or the predominant time zone of their primary team if working across regional borders.

During these core hours, all team members—whether working from home or in the office—must remain logged into official Acme Corp communication channels, including company messaging platforms, calendar systems, and corporate email. Employees are expected to promptly respond to incoming messages, attend scheduled team stand-ups, and participate actively in collaborative project meetings. Outside of core collaboration hours, employees possess schedule flexibility to organize their remaining working hours around personal routines, provided that their total contracted daily and weekly commitments are fully completed.

## Home Office Equipment and Workspace Standards

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a dedicated, ergonomic, and quiet workspace that allows them to perform their professional obligations comfortably and securely. Acme Corp provides full-time remote and hybrid personnel with standard computing equipment, including a managed corporate laptop, external display accessories, and necessary peripheral devices configured with standard enterprise software and security controls.

The home workspace must offer adequate privacy to prevent unauthorized individuals, visitors, or household members from viewing confidential client documents or listening to sensitive business discussions. Employees must ensure that their residential internet connection possesses sufficient bandwidth and stability to maintain clear audio and video connections during virtual group meetings and screen-sharing sessions.

## Communication and Performance Alignment

Remote work performance at Acme Corp is evaluated on tangible deliverables, output quality, and proactive team collaboration rather than mere physical presence. Team leaders conduct regular check-ins and performance discussions to evaluate progress against quarterly goals, assess workload distribution, and address any impediments encountered in remote setups.

Team members are required to maintain an accurate and up-to-date shared calendar, indicating their scheduled in-office days, remote work days, and any approved absences. Whenever an unexpected outage, technical failure, or household emergency disrupts an employee's ability to work during remote hours, they must alert their supervisor immediately through backup communication channels so that alternate arrangements can be made.
