# Acme Corp Business Travel and Expense Policy

Acme Corp is committed to conducting business ethically, efficiently, and responsibly. We recognize that business travel, client engagement, and external conferences are indispensable to expanding our enterprise relationships, driving revenue growth, and advancing our technological leadership. The Acme Corp Business Travel and Expense Policy establishes clear rules and standards governing the authorization, expenditure, reporting, and reimbursement of business-related costs incurred on behalf of the organization.

The overarching philosophy of this policy is based on reasonable judgment, fiscal prudence, and mutual fairness: employees traveling on company business should neither suffer personal financial disadvantage nor experience financial windfall as a result of their service. Every team member has an ongoing obligation to exercise good stewardship over Acme Corp resources, selecting cost-effective travel and entertainment options that align with prudent business practices.

## Policy Scope and Employee Responsibilities

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees, managers, executives, and temporary contractors traveling on approved company business or incurring operational expenses on behalf of Acme Corp. Before making any non-refundable travel bookings or incurring significant expenditure, employees must secure prior written authorization from their respective cost center manager or department director.

Every employee who incurs business expenditures is personally responsible for adhering to company spending thresholds, retaining valid supporting documentation, and submitting complete, truthful expense reports in a timely manner. Approving managers bear joint fiduciary responsibility for reviewing each expense line item, validating legitimate business purpose, confirming budget availability, and ensuring strict adherence to the standards codified within this document.

## Travel Accommodations and Transportation

All business flights, rail transit, and vehicle rentals should be booked well in advance through Acme Corp's designated corporate travel management platform or preferred booking partners. For air travel, economy class is the required standard for all domestic and short-haul international flights lasting under six hours. For continuous flights exceeding six hours in flight duration, premium economy travel may be authorized with written pre-approval from an executive vice president.

Hotel accommodations should be selected from preferred business-class hotels offering reasonable corporate negotiated rates. Employees are encouraged to choose standard single rooms situated in safe, convenient locations near client sites or event venues. Personal incidental charges, including in-room entertainment, minibar snacks, laundry services for trips under five days, and personal telephone calls, are not reimbursable and must be settled independently by the traveler upon checkout.

## Meal Allowances and Daily Limits

Acme Corp provides fair and consistent meal reimbursement to staff who are required to travel away from their standard home work location on business assignments. Under our travel guidelines, Meals are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day on business trips. This daily cap covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and non-alcoholic beverages consumed throughout the travel itinerary.

The daily meal allowance is intended to accommodate customary meal expenses in standard business travel destinations. In high-cost metropolitan cities designated by Acme Corp Finance (such as New York, London, Tokyo, or San Francisco), employees may seek prior approval from their department head for an exceptional daily meal cap of up to 85 USD per day. Alcoholic beverages consumed during individual travel meals are not eligible for reimbursement; however, modest alcohol charges incurred during authorized client entertainment dinners may be reimbursed under the separate client entertainment budget guidelines.

## Receipt Requirements and Itemization

To comply with statutory financial accounting standards, tax audit requirements, and corporate governance regulations, all expenses reported for reimbursement must be substantiated by authentic supporting documents. Specifically, receipts are required above 25 USD for any individual expense item or business transaction. Any expenditure exceeding 25 USD submitted without an attached itemized receipt will be declined for reimbursement by Accounts Payable, except in extraordinary verified cases where receipts could not be obtained due to vendor limitations.

Valid receipts must clearly indicate the vendor name, the transaction date, the physical location, the itemized breakdown of goods or services purchased, and the total payment amount including applicable taxes. Credit card summary slips that do not display individual itemized charges are insufficient for verification and must be accompanied by detailed itemized invoices.

## Claim Deadlines and Submission Process

Timely expense submission is critical for accurate financial reporting, budgetary control, and prompt payroll reimbursement. Employees must submit their expense reports via the online Acme Corp Expense Portal as soon as possible after completing travel. Under corporate financial rules, claims are due within 30 days of the expense transaction date or the conclusion of the business trip.

Expense reports submitted past the mandatory 30-day window will be rejected by the system unless accompanied by an explicit written exception waiver signed by the Chief Financial Officer. Approved expense reports are processed on the bi-weekly corporate payroll cycle, and reimbursement funds are disbursed directly into the employee's designated direct deposit bank account.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses and Audits

Acme Corp explicitly prohibits the reimbursement of personal, non-business related expenditures under any circumstances. Examples of non-reimbursable items include traffic or parking fines, speeding tickets, airline club lounge memberships, flight upgrades purchased at personal discretion, lost personal luggage, spa treatments, tobacco products, and personal clothing purchases.

The Internal Audit and Corporate Finance teams perform routine and random forensic audits on submitted expense claims to ensure policy compliance. Any intentional falsification of receipts, misrepresentation of expense purpose, duplicate claiming, or intentional inflation of costs constitutes gross misconduct that may result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination of employment and civil recovery proceedings.
