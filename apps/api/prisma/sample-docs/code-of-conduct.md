# Acme Corp Corporate Code of Business Conduct and Ethics

Acme Corp was founded on the fundamental principle that sustainable commercial success and technological innovation must always be guided by unquestionable ethical standards, honesty, accountability, and mutual respect. This Corporate Code of Business Conduct and Ethics establishes the behavioral expectations, governance principles, and legal baselines that guide every director, officer, manager, employee, and external representative acting on behalf of Acme Corp worldwide.

We believe that integrity is not merely a legal compliance requirement, but rather the cornerstone of trust between our company, our customers, our partners, and the global communities in which we operate. Every member of the Acme Corp team has a continuous obligation to uphold these values in daily decision-making, in interactions with colleagues, and when delivering products and services to our enterprise clients.

## Standards of Professional Integrity

Professional integrity requires that all employees conduct business with uncompromised honesty, equity, and transparency. At Acme Corp, we compete vigorously and fairly in the marketplace, relying on product superiority, engineering excellence, and customer service rather than deceptive, unfair, or corrupt practices. Team members must never engage in misleading communications, false advertising, or unethical market manipulation.

All corporate records, financial accounts, time tracking sheets, research reports, and official filings must reflect complete, accurate, and timely truth. Falsifying documents, obscuring records, creating unrecorded off-the-books funds, or misrepresenting financial metrics to auditors or regulatory bodies violates company rules and applicable corporate governance laws, exposing violators to severe personal liability.

## Workplace Respect and Anti-Harassment

Acme Corp is dedicated to maintaining an inclusive, diverse, professional, and psychologically safe workplace where every individual is treated with dignity, courtesy, and fairness. Discrimination on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected under applicable law is strictly prohibited across all operations and working environments.

Harassment in any form—whether verbal, physical, written, or visual—will not be tolerated. This prohibition encompasses unwelcome sexual advances, offensive jokes or derogatory slurs, hostile remarks, intimidation, bullying, and any unwelcome behavior that creates an intimidating, degrading, or abusive work atmosphere. Managers are required to actively promote an inclusive environment and intervene immediately whenever unprofessional behavior is observed or reported.

## Gifts, Entertainment, and Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations in which their personal, social, or financial relationships create—or appear to create—a conflict with the best business interests of Acme Corp. A conflict of interest arises whenever personal judgment or loyalty is compromised by private gain, outside commercial ties, or secondary business employment.

In the course of business relationships, exchanging customary business courtesies such as occasional meals or modest promotional items can build goodwill. However, accepting gifts that could influence or appear to influence commercial decisions is strictly forbidden. Under Acme Corp compliance rules, gifts above 50 USD must be declared formally through the internal compliance registry and reviewed by the Legal and Ethics Committee. Cash, gift cards, loans, or securities may never be offered or accepted under any circumstances, regardless of amount.

## Reporting Channels and Ethics Contact

Maintaining an open and ethical corporate culture relies on the willingness of employees to voice concerns, seek guidance, and report suspected wrongdoings promptly. If an employee becomes aware of any actual or suspected breach of this Code, company policies, accounting controls, or applicable laws, they are expected to report the matter immediately.

Several accessible reporting channels are available. Employees may discuss concerns directly with their line supervisor, departmental director, or Human Resources representative. For formal, confidential, or sensitive inquiries, Concerns are reported to ethics@acme.example, which is monitored directly by the Acme Corp Compliance and Ethics Committee. Alternatively, reports may be submitted through our independent, confidential reporting hotline, which is operated twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, and supports anonymous submissions where permitted by local law.

## Protection Against Retaliation

Acme Corp strictly protects any individual who speaks up in good faith to report potential misconduct, raise safety concerns, or participate in an ethical investigation. Under our explicit whistleblower policy, retaliation is prohibited against any employee who reports a known or suspected violation, assists an internal or external regulatory investigation, or refuses to participate in unethical or unlawful directives.

Retaliation can take many forms, including subtle workplace ostracism, negative performance appraisals unsupported by facts, demotion, suspension, denial of promotions, reduction in work hours, or termination of employment. Any manager or team member found to have engaged in retaliatory behavior against a colleague will face immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. If you believe you have experienced retaliation for raising a concern, notify the Ethics Committee at once.

## Investigation and Disciplinary Measures

All reports received through official ethics channels are logged, evaluated, and investigated promptly, thoroughly, and impartially by trained compliance professionals or independent legal counsel. The company strives to protect the confidentiality of the reporting individual and all persons involved in the inquiry to the greatest extent possible under applicable legal mandates.

Violations of this Code of Business Conduct and Ethics erode team trust and undermine organizational health. Where an investigation substantiates that a policy violation has occurred, Acme Corp will take prompt, appropriate corrective and disciplinary measures. Depending on the gravity and nature of the infraction, disciplinary sanctions may range from formal written warnings, mandatory compliance retraining, and reassignment, to forfeiture of incentive bonuses, suspension, and termination of employment for cause.
